## Driving Notes
Dublin → Drogheda: ~45 min (50km). Add stops along the coast road. Don't overload — this is also a driving day.

## Contingency
Newgrange is the anchor — prioritise booking. Other Drogheda museums are flexible.

## Open Questions

- Carrickmacross is slightly off the main Dublin–Drogheda route (~30 min detour) — worth it per the doc.
