## Leg 1 — Dublin (Jul 13–16)

Base: [Hendrick Smithfield](logistics/hendrick-smithfield.md), Smithfield · [Leg 1 itinerary](itinerary/leg-1-dublin.md)

### Dublinia ✅

Interactive museum covering Viking and medieval Dublin, housed in a Gothic synod hall connected to Christ Church Cathedral. Hands-on exhibits include a Viking village, a plague doctor role-play, and a medieval medicine box. One of the more engaging history museums in Dublin for children.

**Cost:** ~€16 adult / ~€10 child · **Duration:** 1.5–2 h · **Age:** 6+ · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** 25 min walk from hotel · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Dublinia+Dublin+Ireland)

### Guinness Storehouse ✅

Seven-floor self-guided experience tracing the history of Guinness from Arthur's 1759 lease through the brewing process to the Gravity Bar with panoramic Dublin views. The lower floors (cooperage, ingredients) are engaging for all ages; the Gravity Bar (complimentary pint included in ticket) is adults only. Allow a full half-day.

**Cost:** ~€30 adult / ~€18 child · **Duration:** 2–2.5 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Pre-book — sells out in summer · **Distance:** 25 min walk from hotel · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Guinness+Storehouse+Dublin+Ireland)

!!! warning "Pre-book"
    This is the single most visited paid attraction in Ireland. Book online well before the trip — July slots fill weeks in advance.

### National Leprechaun Museum ✅

Storytelling-led tour through Irish mythology and folklore, with rooms designed at giant-scale to make visitors feel small. Aimed squarely at families with younger children. Evening "Dark Folklore" tours are adults-only and more atmospheric.

**Cost:** ~€18 adult / ~€13 child · **Duration:** 1 h · **Age:** 4–10 best · **Booking:** Recommended · **Distance:** 15 min walk from hotel · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=National+Leprechaun+Museum+Dublin+Ireland)

### World of Illusion 🔄

Three-storey interactive attraction in Temple Bar with 70+ exhibits covering light, science, and perception — vortex tunnels, infinity mirrors, gravity rooms, and camera-trick photo opportunities. Good value on a rainy afternoon and can be done in under 2 hours.

**Cost:** ~€20 adult / ~€15 child · **Duration:** 1.5 h · **Age:** 5+ · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** 20 min walk from hotel · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=World+of+Illusion+Dublin)

### Chester Beatty Museum 🔄

Free world-class museum with manuscripts, miniature paintings, decorative arts, and religious texts from across Asia, the Middle East, and Europe. Rotating family workshops on topics like silk weaving and calligraphy. One of the most consistently praised free attractions in Dublin.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** 1–2 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** 20 min walk from hotel · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Chester+Beatty+Library+Dublin)

### National Museum of Ireland — Archaeology 🔄

Ireland's archaeology collection, entirely free. Highlights include bog bodies (Iron Age human remains preserved in peat), Viking-age gold hoards, and the Tara Brooch. The bog body gallery is fascinating for older kids; younger ones often enjoy the scale of the Viking material.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** 1–2 h · **Age:** 8+ for bog bodies · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** 30 min walk from hotel · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=National+Museum+of+Ireland+Archaeology+Dublin)

### Dream Point Experience 🔄

Ireland's largest indoor playground — 21,000 sq ft with the country's biggest ball pit (250,000 balls), a balloon room, and an immersive projection room. Better for younger children than older ones; good wet-day anchor for under-10s.

**Cost:** ~€15–18 · **Duration:** 2 h · **Age:** 3–10 best · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** City centre · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Dream+Point+Dublin)

### The Ark 🔄

Purpose-built cultural centre for children aged 2–12, in Temple Bar. Runs workshops, performances, and hands-on art sessions throughout the year. Check the schedule before visiting as programming varies.

**Cost:** Varies by programme · **Duration:** 1–2 h · **Age:** 2–12 · **Booking:** Check schedule and book in advance · **Distance:** 20 min walk from hotel · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=The+Ark+Dublin)

### Aquazone — National Aquatic Centre 🔄

Full indoor waterpark with water slides (including the Boomerang and drop slides), a wave machine, and a pirate-themed splash area for younger children. Under-3s free. Gets busy on summer weekends — go on a weekday morning if possible.

**Cost:** ~€20 adult / ~€16 child / free under-3 · **Duration:** 2–3 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~20 min drive to Blanchardstown · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Aquazone+National+Aquatic+Centre+Dublin)

## Leg 2 — Drogheda (Jul 16–17)

Base: [Scholars Townhouse Hotel](logistics/scholars-townhouse-hotel.md), Drogheda · [Leg 2 itinerary](itinerary/leg-2-drogheda.md)

### Ardgillan Castle and Garden ✅

18th-century castle with terraced gardens and walled kitchen garden overlooking the Irish Sea, in Balbriggan. The grounds are free and make a good leg-stretch on the drive north from Dublin. Castle interior tours run in summer (check hours). On the M1/N1 route, easy to stop without adding much time.

**Cost:** Grounds free; castle tour ~€6 · **Duration:** 45 min–1 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** En route — 30 km north of Dublin · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Ardgillan+Castle+Balbriggan+Ireland)

See also: [Ardgillan Castle](places/ardgillan.md)

### Carrickmacross Workhouse 🔄

**Plan B** — dropped from the Leg 2 day plan as a 40 km wrong-direction detour off the Dublin → Drogheda route, but kept here as an optional stop. Famine-era workhouse museum in Co. Monaghan. Documents the Irish Famine (1845–52) through the experience of a local workhouse — original buildings, interpretive displays, and personal accounts. A good kid-friendly option if the day has slack. Allows a real conversation about a defining moment in Irish history. Also, Carrickmacross is famous for its lace; shops nearby.

**Cost:** Low / free · **Duration:** 45 min–1 h · **Age:** 8+ · **Booking:** Check hours before visiting · **Distance:** ~30 min detour off the N2/M2 · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Carrickmacross+Workhouse+Monaghan+Ireland)

!!! note "Detour note"
    This is off the direct Dublin → Drogheda route. Worth it per the planning doc, but check opening hours — smaller heritage sites can have limited summer schedules.

See also: [Carrickmacross Workhouse](places/carrickmacross.md)

### Brú na Bóinne / Newgrange ✅

UNESCO World Heritage passage tomb complex built ~3200 BC — older than Stonehenge and the Pyramids of Giza. Access is through the Brú na Bóinne Visitor Centre only; shuttle buses run to the monuments. The combined Newgrange + Knowth ticket gives the best value and the most complete experience. The interior of Newgrange (roof-box aligned with the winter solstice sunrise) is genuinely extraordinary.

**Cost:** Adult ~€22 / Child ~€13 (combined ticket) · **Duration:** 2.5–3 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Must pre-book — sells out weeks ahead in summer · **Distance:** 8 km west of Drogheda · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Bru+na+Boinne+Visitor+Centre+Donore+Ireland)

!!! warning "Pre-book now"
    Brú na Bóinne is one of the most popular heritage sites in Ireland. July slots sell out far in advance — book on the OPW website as soon as possible.

See also: [Newgrange / Brú na Bóinne](places/newgrange.md)

### Drogheda Museum — Millmount ✅

18th-century Martello-style tower and museum complex in the heart of Drogheda, with exhibits on local history, Cromwellian sieges, and trade guilds. Good compact museum for an hour. The tower itself offers views over town.

**Cost:** ~€5 adult / €3 child · **Duration:** 1 h · **Age:** 8+ · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** In Drogheda town · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Millmount+Museum+Drogheda+Ireland)

### Irish Military War Museum ✅

Small private collection of military memorabilia — uniforms, weapons, vehicles, and photographs spanning Irish military history from the 1916 Rising through to recent peacekeeping. Niche but engaging for history-minded visitors and older kids with an interest in military history.

**Cost:** ~€8 adult / €5 child · **Duration:** 1 h · **Age:** 10+ · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** Drogheda, 5 min from hotel · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Irish+Military+War+Museum+Drogheda+Ireland)

### Old Mellifont Abbey ✅

First Cistercian monastery in Ireland, founded 1142. Atmospheric ruins beside the River Mattock — lavabo (washing hall), chapter house, and gatehouse survive. Quiet, largely crowd-free site. Good for a short stop.

**Cost:** ~€5 / family €13 · **Duration:** 45 min · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** 10 km west of Drogheda · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Old+Mellifont+Abbey+Collon+Ireland)

### Funtasia Drogheda 🔄

Large indoor entertainment complex — waterpark with slides (Boomerang, Super Bowl), bowling alley, zipline, crazy golf, and arcade. The strongest rainy-day option if Newgrange is sold out or the afternoon needs filling. Gets very busy on wet Irish days, so arrive early.

**Cost:** ~€25–30 (waterpark + activities bundle) · **Duration:** 2–3 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** In Drogheda, 10 min from hotel · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Funtasia+Drogheda+Ireland)

### Battle of the Boyne — Oldbridge Estate 🔄

Visitor centre with interactive exhibits about the 1690 Battle of the Boyne (William III vs James II), set on the actual battlefield site. Good for history-minded adults and teenagers; parkland around the site is pleasant for a walk.

**Cost:** ~€5 adult / free under-12 · **Duration:** 1–1.5 h · **Age:** 10+ for exhibits · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** 5 km south of Drogheda · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Battle+of+the+Boyne+Oldbridge+Ireland)

### Carlingford 🔄

Beautifully preserved medieval walled town on the Cooley Peninsula on Carlingford Lough, with views into the Mourne Mountains. King John's Castle (12th century) is free to view from outside. The Carlingford Adventure Centre offers kayaking on the lough, a skypark, abseiling, and water trampolining — good active half-day for kids.

**Cost:** Town free; adventure centre activities from ~€25 pp · **Duration:** Half day · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Adventure centre: book ahead · **Distance:** ~40 min north of Drogheda · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Carlingford+Co+Louth+Ireland)

### Laytown / Bettystown Beach 🔄

Sandy east-coast beach just off the M1. Good for a short stop en route. Laytown is known for its annual horse races run directly on the beach (not July). Nothing elaborate — just a stretch and a paddle.

**Cost:** Free (parking fee) · **Duration:** 30–60 min · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~15 min east of Drogheda · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Laytown+Beach+Co+Meath+Ireland)

## Leg 3 / Leg 4 — Northern Ireland (Jul 17–20)

Base: [Hampton by Hilton Belfast City Centre](logistics/hampton-by-hilton-belfast.md) · [Leg 3 itinerary](itinerary/leg-3-northern-ireland.md) · [Leg 4 itinerary](itinerary/leg-4-belfast.md)

Distances below are from Belfast city centre. GoT Studio Tour and Bagenal Castle are ~40 min south (also reachable en route from Drogheda on Jul 17).

### Game of Thrones Studio Tour ✅

The official Game of Thrones production studio in Banbridge, where much of the HBO series was filmed. Full original sets, costumes, props, and weapons, plus green-screen experiences. One of the most immersive TV/film experiences in the UK or Ireland. Family admission covers 2 adults + 2 children; age-appropriateness depends on GoT familiarity.

**Cost:** ~£64 for family / ~£29 adult · **Duration:** 3–4 h · **Age:** teens best; 10+ with parental call on content · **Booking:** Must pre-book · **Distance:** ~40 km south of Belfast city centre (~40 min) · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Game+of+Thrones+Studio+Tour+Banbridge+Northern+Ireland)

!!! warning "Pre-book"
    Day trip from Belfast on Jul 18, or do en route from Drogheda on Jul 17. Check availability and book early — July fills fast.

### Bagenal Castle, Newry ✅

16th-century castle housing the Newry and Mourne Museum, with exhibits from prehistoric times through to the present day — strong on local folklore, the linen trade, and Famine history. The castle itself (one of Ireland's oldest surviving urban castles) is atmospheric. Good compact stop if arriving via Newry.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** 1 h · **Age:** 8+ · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~45 min south of Belfast (Newry city) · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Bagenal+Castle+Newry+Northern+Ireland)

### Downpatrick ✅ (contingency)

Small town in Co. Down with strong St Patrick connections. Down Cathedral contains the reputed grave of St Patrick, marked by a large rough stone. The adjacent Down County Museum (in an 18th-century gaol) covers 9,000 years of local history and includes a replica Victorian cell block. Recommended contingency if GoT Studio Tour is unavailable.

**Cost:** Museum free; Cathedral free · **Duration:** 1.5–2 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~40 min from Belfast city centre · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Downpatrick+Northern+Ireland)

### Tollymore Forest Park 🔄

630-hectare forest park at the foot of the Mourne Mountains — and a genuine GoT filming location (the Haunted Forest in season 1). Waymarked trails through ancient oaks and along the Shimna River; stone arched bridges; accessible for all fitness levels. Free entry. A GoT consolation prize that's also genuinely beautiful.

**Cost:** Car parking fee only (~£5) · **Duration:** 1.5–2 h walk · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~55 min from Belfast via Newcastle · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Tollymore+Forest+Park+Newcastle+Northern+Ireland)

### Slieve Gullion Forest Park 🔄

Forest park on the slopes of Slieve Gullion, a volcanic mountain in Co. Armagh. Fionn MacCumhaill's giant adventure trail with themed play areas suits younger kids well; the hilltop walk (challenging, 2 h) offers views across Armagh and Down. Strong Irish mythology connections.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** 1.5–3 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~55 min south of Belfast · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Slieve+Gullion+Forest+Park+Northern+Ireland)

### Narnia Trail — Kilbroney Park, Rostrevor 🔄

Woodland trail through ancient oak forest in Kilbroney Park, with sculptures and scenes from C.S. Lewis's *The Chronicles of Narnia* (Lewis drew on the Mournes landscape for Narnia). Easy family walk, 1–2 km. Rostrevor village itself is charming. Combine with a walk along the seafront promenade on Carlingford Lough.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** 1 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~50 min south of Belfast · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Kilbroney+Park+Rostrevor+Northern+Ireland)

### Delamont Country Park 🔄

Family-friendly park on the shores of Strangford Lough with a miniature railway, walking trails, orienteering, and wildlife watching. Gentle and relaxed — good option if the group needs a low-key afternoon. Views across the lough.

**Cost:** Car parking fee · **Duration:** 1.5–2 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~40 min from Belfast (Strangford Lough) · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Delamont+Country+Park+Killyleagh+Northern+Ireland)

## Leg 4 — Belfast city (Jul 17–20)

Base: [Hampton by Hilton Belfast City Centre](logistics/hampton-by-hilton-belfast.md) · [Leg 4 itinerary](itinerary/leg-4-belfast.md)

### Titanic Belfast ✅

The world's largest Titanic visitor experience, built on the very slipway where the ship was constructed. Nine galleries covering the ship's design, construction, launch, and sinking, with a dark ride through a recreated shipyard and original underwater footage of the wreck. Genuinely excellent — one of the best museum experiences in the British Isles.

**Cost:** ~£22 adult / £11 child · **Duration:** 2.5–3 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Pre-book — sells out in summer · **Distance:** 15–20 min walk or short taxi from city centre · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Titanic+Belfast+Northern+Ireland)

!!! warning "Pre-book"
    This is the anchor for Day 1 in Belfast. Book well ahead — July is peak season.

See also: [Belfast](places/belfast.md)

### SS Nomadic ✅

The last surviving White Star Line vessel — Titanic's tender ship that ferried first and second class passengers out to Titanic in Cherbourg. Moored immediately adjacent to Titanic Belfast. Self-guided tour through all three decks. Best done as part of the same Titanic Quarter visit.

**Cost:** ~£9 adult / £5 child (or combined ticket with Titanic Belfast) · **Duration:** 1 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Include in Titanic Belfast booking · **Distance:** Beside Titanic Belfast · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=SS+Nomadic+Belfast+Northern+Ireland)

### Ulster Museum ✅

Free national museum covering art, history, and natural history. Kid highlights: the Egyptian mummy, dinosaur bones, the Girona Armada gold collection, and the art galleries. Large enough for a half-day; can be combined with the adjacent Botanic Gardens.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** 1.5–2.5 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** 10–15 min from city centre · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Ulster+Museum+Belfast+Northern+Ireland)

### Ulster Folk Museum ✅

Open-air museum in Cultra (~20 min from city) where historic Ulster buildings have been dismantled and rebuilt on-site — farmhouses, cottages, a school, a forge, a church. Costumed interpreters demonstrate traditional crafts. Give it a full morning; kids respond well to the open format and the animals.

**Cost:** ~£12 adult / £7 child · **Duration:** 2.5–3 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** 20 min drive to Cultra · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Ulster+Folk+Museum+Cultra+Northern+Ireland)

### W5 ✅

Interactive science and discovery centre in the Odyssey complex — hands-on exhibits across five themed zones covering technology, engineering, nature, and creativity. The consistently top-rated family attraction in Belfast for the 5–12 age range. Allow 2–3 hours.

**Cost:** ~£14 adult / £10 child · **Duration:** 2–3 h · **Age:** 5–12 best · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** Beside Titanic Belfast (Odyssey) · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=W5+Belfast+Northern+Ireland)

### Belfast Castle ✅

Victorian baronial castle on the Cave Hill — worth visiting for the views over Belfast and the Lough as much as the castle itself. The cave walk up to McArt's Fort (where Wolfe Tone is said to have made his oath) adds an hour and is a proper hill walk. The grounds and café are free; castle interior houses a restaurant.

**Cost:** Grounds free; parking ~£3 · **Duration:** 1.5–2 h (+ cave hill walk) · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** 20 min drive north · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Belfast+Castle+Northern+Ireland)

### Carrickfergus Castle ✅

One of the best-preserved Norman castles in Ireland, dating from 1177, set dramatically on a promontory on the north shore of Belfast Lough. Kids can explore towers, a great hall, and a dungeon. Combination of castle + short coastal drive makes this a strong half-day trip.

**Cost:** ~£6 adult / £3 child · **Duration:** 1–1.5 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** 20 min north of Belfast · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Carrickfergus+Castle+Northern+Ireland)

### Belfast Cathedral (St Anne's) ✅

Anglican cathedral in the Cathedral Quarter, completed in stages from 1904 to 2007. Notable for its mosaics, the baptistry dome, and the spire of hope added in 2007. Short visit; good as a quick cultural stop combined with the Cathedral Quarter and the nearby PS I Love You street art.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** 30–45 min · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** City centre, ~5 min walk from hotel · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=St+Anne+Cathedral+Belfast+Northern+Ireland)

### Belfast Black Cab Tour 🔄

~1-hour guided tour of the West Belfast political murals on the Falls Road (republican) and Shankill Road (loyalist). Consistently the single most-recommended Belfast experience on travel forums (TripAdvisor, Reddit). Guides are typically former residents of the area and present both sides. Adults and older teenagers — not for younger children.

**Cost:** ~£35–50 per cab (group) · **Duration:** 1–1.5 h · **Age:** 12+ · **Booking:** Book in advance via local operators · **Distance:** City centre pick-up · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Black+Cab+Tours+Belfast+Northern+Ireland)

### Botanic Gardens 🔄

Free public gardens immediately adjacent to the Ulster Museum. The Victorian Palm House (a cast-iron glasshouse from 1840) and the Tropical Ravine are both worth 20 minutes. Good lunch-break space if visiting the Ulster Museum — pick up food nearby and eat on the lawns.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** 30–60 min · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** Beside Ulster Museum · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Botanic+Gardens+Belfast+Northern+Ireland)

### Titanic Quarter Self-Guided Walk 🔄

Free walking route around the historic dock area — the actual slipways where Titanic and Olympic were built (now marked with outlines), the Hamilton Graving Dock (dry dock used for fitting out), the Pump House, and HMS Caroline (a WWI cruiser). A good supplement or alternative if Titanic Belfast is already booked for another day.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** 1–1.5 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** Titanic Quarter · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Titanic+Quarter+Belfast+Northern+Ireland)

### Lost City Adventure Golf 🔄

Indoor themed adventure golf with two 18-hole courses, special effects, and interactive obstacles. Good rainy-day fallback or early-evening activity before dinner. Central location.

**Cost:** ~£10 per round · **Duration:** 1–1.5 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** City centre · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Lost+City+Adventure+Golf+Belfast+Northern+Ireland)

### Exploris Aquarium & Seal Sanctuary 🔄

Northern Ireland's only public aquarium in Portaferry on the Strangford Lough narrows. Native Irish Sea species alongside tropical tanks; the seal sanctuary rescues and rehabilitates seals (not always on public view, but sometimes visible). Getting here involves the Strangford ferry crossing (~8 min), which is fun in itself. Combine with Mount Stewart for a full day.

**Cost:** ~£12 adult / £8 child · **Duration:** 1.5–2 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~45 min east via Strangford ferry · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Exploris+Aquarium+Portaferry+Northern+Ireland)

### Mount Stewart — National Trust 🔄

Early 20th-century country house with award-winning formal gardens on the shores of Strangford Lough. Lady Londonderry created a series of themed garden rooms — the Sunken Garden, the Spanish Garden, a dodo terrace with animal topiary. House tours available. Combine with Exploris for a full east-Down day.

**Cost:** ~£14 adult / £7 child (NT members free) · **Duration:** 2 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~35 min east of Belfast · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Mount+Stewart+Northern+Ireland)

### Cave Hill Country Park 🔄

Free country park encompassing Belfast Castle, the Cave Hill (368m), and views over the whole city and Belfast Lough. The full walk to McArt's Fort takes about 2.5 hours return — rewarding but challenging. Even just the castle grounds and lower slopes are worth an hour.

**Cost:** Free (parking ~£3) · **Duration:** 1–3 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** 20 min drive north · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Cave+Hill+Country+Park+Belfast+Northern+Ireland)

### Emerald Park ✅

Ireland's largest theme park and zoo (formerly Tayto Park), in Ashbourne, Co. Meath. Rollercoasters including the Cú Chulainn Coaster, a zoo with giraffe feeding, fairground rides, and water attractions. Plan for a full day — 6–8 hours. Peak Irish school holiday season in July; expect queues for the major rides.

**Cost:** ~€40 adult / €35 child (20% off if booked the day before) · **Duration:** Full day · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Book on Jul 20 for 20% discount — do not leave to the day · **Distance:** ~25 min from Leixlip hotel · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Emerald+Park+Ashbourne+Meath+Ireland)

!!! warning "Book on July 20"
    The 20% early-booking discount only applies when booking in advance. On the evening of July 20 (after checking into the Courtyard Hotel), book the tickets for July 21 online.

See also: [Emerald Park](places/emerald-park.md)

### St Catherine's Park 🔄

Riverside park immediately adjacent to the Courtyard Hotel — good for a free, gentle walk on the arrival afternoon. Follows the Liffey through open parkland. No facilities other than trails. Good for a leg-stretch after a 2-hour drive from Belfast.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** 30–60 min · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** Beside the hotel · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=St+Catherines+Park+Leixlip+Ireland)

### Fort Lucan Adventureland 🔄

Outdoor activity park in a wild-west theme — water zorbing, go-karts, assault courses, a dedicated toddler village, and zip lines. Strong alternative to Emerald Park if the latter is closed or over-budget; could also work as a shorter activity on a spare afternoon. Weather dependent.

**Cost:** ~€15–20 pp · **Duration:** 2–3 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~15 min east (near Lucan) · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Fort+Lucan+Adventureland+Dublin+Ireland)

### Clonfert Pet Farm, Maynooth 🔄

Animal farm with meerkats, llamas, arctic foxes, pygmy goats, and more. Three playgrounds, go-karts, and indoor play. Good for younger children (under 8 especially). Less intense than a theme park — good Jul 20 afternoon option.

**Cost:** ~€13 pp · **Duration:** 2 h · **Age:** 2–8 best · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~15 min · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Clonfert+Pet+Farm+Maynooth+Ireland)

### Kildare Maze, Prosperous 🔄

Ireland's largest hedge maze — 2 km of paths with a viewing tower at the centre. On-site extras include a zip wire, adventure trail, and crazy golf. Works well as a 2-hour afternoon activity.

**Cost:** ~€12 adult / €10 child · **Duration:** 1.5–2 h · **Age:** 5+ · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~30 min south · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Kildare+Maze+Prosperous+Ireland)

### Dublin Zoo & Phoenix Park 🔄

One of Europe's oldest and most-visited zoos. Phoenix Park is one of the largest enclosed city parks in Europe (1,750 acres) — deer graze freely in the park. Good half-day if the group wants a quieter alternative to Emerald Park, or as an add-on the morning before checking out.

**Cost:** ~€23 adult / €16 child · **Duration:** 3–4 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Recommended in peak season · **Distance:** ~15 min east · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Dublin+Zoo+Phoenix+Park+Ireland)

## Leg 5 — Wicklow / Druid's Glen (Jul 20–22)

Base: [Druids Glen Resort](logistics/druids-glen-resort.md) · [Leg 5 itinerary](itinerary/leg-6-wicklow.md)

Note: 2 nights (Jul 20–22). Jul 21 is a resort / Wicklow Mountains day; on Jul 22 pick **one** of these en route to Dublin Airport.

### Glendalough ✅

Early Christian monastic settlement founded by St Kevin in the 6th century, set in a glacial valley between two lakes in the Wicklow Mountains. The iconic Round Tower, cathedral ruins, and St Kevin's Kitchen (a small oratory) date from the 8th–12th centuries. Can get crowded mid-summer midday — arrive early or late afternoon.

**Cost:** Free (parking fee applies) · **Duration:** 2–3 h · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~20 min from Druids Glen · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Glendalough+Visitor+Centre+Wicklow+Ireland)

The **Upper Lake trail** (~1 h loop) is the most scenic walk — passes the beehive hermitages, the upper lake shoreline, and offers mountain views. The lower monastic site is accessible and takes about 45 minutes to walk through.

See also: [Glendalough](places/glendalough.md)

### Wicklow Mountains National Park ✅

The wider national park surrounding Glendalough — the Sally Gap, Glenmacnass Waterfall, Luggala, and the Military Road (R115) form a dramatic mountain drive. Combining Glendalough with a drive through the park adds minimal time but significantly more scenery.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** Add 1–2 h to the Glendalough visit · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** Surrounding Glendalough · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Wicklow+Mountains+National+Park+Ireland)

### Powerscourt Estate & Gardens 🔄

One of Ireland's great formal gardens — Italianate terraces descending to a central fountain, the Sugar Loaf Mountain as the backdrop. The estate includes garden rooms, a Japanese garden, a pet cemetery, and a garden centre/café. The separate Powerscourt Waterfall (Ireland's highest, 121m) is 6 km away and worth the detour for a short walk.

**Cost:** Gardens ~€12 adult / €7 child; Waterfall ~€8 adult / €5 child · **Duration:** 2 h (gardens) + 1 h (waterfall) · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~30 min north of Druids Glen · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Powerscourt+Estate+Gardens+Enniskerry+Ireland)

!!! note "Best combo for Wicklow"
    Glendalough in the morning (arrive early, beat the crowd) + Avoca village for lunch + Powerscourt Gardens in the afternoon is a strong full-day Wicklow circuit.

### Avoca Handweavers 🔄

The world's oldest surviving working woollen mill (established 1723) in the village of Avoca. The mill shop sells knitwear and textiles made on-site; the café above is well-regarded. The village itself, at the confluence of the Avoca and Avonmore rivers, is peaceful and photogenic. Directly en route between Glendalough and Druids Glen.

**Cost:** Free (shop + mill viewing) · **Duration:** 45 min · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~10 min south of Glendalough · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Avoca+Handweavers+Avoca+Wicklow+Ireland)

### Vale of Avoca — Meeting of the Waters 🔄

Short, scenic stop at the confluence of the Avonmore and Avoca rivers — the spot that inspired Thomas Moore's poem "The Meeting of the Waters." Roadside pull-in; takes 15 minutes. Good as a bridge stop between Glendalough and Druids Glen.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** 15 min · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~15 min south of Glendalough · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Meeting+of+the+Waters+Avoca+Wicklow+Ireland)

### Wicklow Gaol 🔄

18th-century county gaol preserved as a museum with guided tours through the cells, dungeons, and exercise yard. Covers the 1798 Rebellion, transportation to Australia, and the Famine period. Dark and atmospheric — good for older children and adults. Avoid with under-8s.

**Cost:** ~€11 adult / €7 child · **Duration:** 1–1.5 h · **Age:** 8+ · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~30 min east (Wicklow town) · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Wicklow+Gaol+Ireland)

### Bray Seafront & Cliff Walk 🔄

Seaside town with a long promenade, arcades, and a beach. The Bray to Greystones Cliff Walk (8 km one way) is one of the best coastal walks in the east of Ireland — clifftop views, accessible path, 2.5 h one way. Good if the Wicklow morning is free before checkout.

**Cost:** Free · **Duration:** 1–3 h depending on how much of the walk you do · **Age:** all · **Booking:** Walk-in · **Distance:** ~35 min north · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Bray+Seafront+Wicklow+Ireland)

### Powerscourt Distillery 🔄

Working whiskey distillery in the Powerscourt Estate grounds. Guided tours include the production floor and maturation warehouse; 60-minute standard tour or 90-minute food-and-folklore option, both ending with a tasting of Fercullen whiskey. Adults only for the tasting; the tour itself is suitable for all ages.

**Cost:** ~€20 (60 min) / €28 (90 min) · **Duration:** 1–1.5 h · **Age:** 18+ for tasting; tour all ages · **Booking:** Book in advance · **Distance:** ~25 min north of Druids Glen (Powerscourt Estate) · [Map](https://maps.google.com/?q=Powerscourt+Distillery+Wicklow+Ireland)
