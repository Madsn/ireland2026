# Ireland 2026 Planning Document

## Summary
Main planning doc from Google Drive shared between the two traveling families (Madsen and Wilhelmsen). Covers the full July 13–23 trip: confirmed hotel bookings for most legs, arrival/departure flights for both families, and a wish list of activities at each stop. One leg (Donegal, Jul 20–21) is flagged for cancellation — to be replaced with a stop closer to Emerald Park.

## Key Information

**Two families**: Madsen and Wilhelmsen traveling together.
**Trip**: July 13–23, 2026 (10 nights).
**Shared map**: https://maps.app.goo.gl/GKdBy42FMzYMUj9b8
**Hotel policy**: breakfast included where possible, 1 castle hotel, budget options, consider parking costs.
