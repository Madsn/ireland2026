    Flights¶

    Both families arrive Dublin on July 13 (~5 hours apart). Madsen departs July 23 to Copenhagen to visit family, then returns to Abu Dhabi on August 3. Wilhelmsen departs Dublin on the same morning of July 23.

    Inbound — July 13¶

    Family Flight Route Departs Arrives DUB Notes
    Wilhelmsen Aer Lingus EI80 — — 08:10 Arrives first — ~5h before Madsen
    Madsen Pegasus PC409 AUH → IST → DUB 03:30 AUH 13:00 Via Istanbul (connecting leg PC1157). PNR: 1RN8BW

    Wilhelmsen arrives ~5 hours before Madsen. Plan for early hotel check-in or luggage storage at Hendrick Smithfield.

    Outbound — July 23¶

    Both families depart Dublin the same morning. Madsen goes to Copenhagen (visiting family in Denmark); Wilhelmsen heads home.

    Family Flight Route Departs DUB Arrives Notes
    Madsen Ryanair FR632 DUB → CPH 11:25 14:35 CPH Reservation: KRE1VY
    Wilhelmsen Aer Lingus EI164 — 12:00 —

    Premier Inn Dublin Airport is the final hotel — proximity to the airport is the point. Leave hotel by 08:30 at latest.
