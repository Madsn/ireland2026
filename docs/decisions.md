Decisions the group needs to make before tickets can be booked. Work through these in order — Decision 1 drives everything else in Belfast.

## Decision 1: Game of Thrones Studio Tour — Jul 17 en route or Jul 18 day trip?

GoT is a confirmed must. The only question is which day. This choice cascades into the entire Belfast schedule.

**Option A — Jul 17 en route from Drogheda**

After Newgrange (done by ~11:30), drive south via Banbridge rather than straight to Belfast.

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| ~11:30 | Depart Drogheda area after Newgrange |
| ~12:40 | Arrive Game of Thrones Studio Tour (Banbridge) |
| ~15:40 | Depart Banbridge |
| ~16:20 | Check in Hampton by Hilton Belfast |

Pros: GoT done on Day 1 of NI, frees both Jul 18 and Jul 19 for pure Belfast.  
Cons: Jul 17 is already a Newgrange morning + long drive day — adding 3h of GoT Studio makes it a full-on day with less time to settle in Belfast. Newgrange → Banbridge → Belfast is roughly 3h of driving total.

**Option B — Jul 18 day trip from Belfast**

Sleep in Belfast on Jul 17, then drive down to Banbridge the next morning.

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| ~09:00 | Drive from Belfast to GoT Studio (~40 min) |
| ~09:40–13:00 | Game of Thrones Studio Tour (3–3.5 h) |
| ~13:30–14:00 | Back in Belfast |
| Afternoon | Combine with one Belfast activity (Ulster Museum is nearby-ish) |

Pros: More relaxed pace. Jul 17 arrival becomes a gentle settle-in afternoon rather than a rushed day.  
Cons: Uses one of the two full Belfast days — the other day (Jul 19) needs to cover Titanic + anything else.

**Recommendation to discuss:** Option B leaves more breathing room, but Option A is completely workable if the group has energy for a big Day 1. Given GoT is an absolute must, Option A removes the risk of any Belfast-day disruption affecting it.

## Decision 3: Belfast Black Cab Tour — yes or no?

A 1.5 h guided tour of the Falls Road and Shankill Road political murals with an ex-driver from one side or the other. One of the most distinctive Belfast experiences. Cost: ~£35–50 per cab (fits the whole family in one cab).

Content is adult — the Troubles, the peace walls, political history. Fine for teenagers; the 8-year-olds will ride along but won't fully engage.

**If yes:** book via [Original Belfast Black Cab Tours](https://belfastcabbietours.com/) or similar. Can be picked up from city centre. Works best as a ~09:00 start before a later-morning activity.

**If no:** skip and use that 1.5 h for extra time at Titanic / W5.

## Decision 5: Newgrange — passage tomb interior or visitor centre only?

[Brú na Bóinne](https://www.heritageireland.ie/places-to-visit/bru-na-boinne/) has two sites: Newgrange (the famous passage tomb) and Knowth (another tomb). Admission is through the visitor centre only — a bus takes you to the sites.

The full Newgrange experience including the interior is the iconic thing to do, but interior access is timed and limited — it sells out faster than the standard ticket. **Must be pre-booked on the [OPW website](https://heritageireland.ie/).**

Decision: book the full interior tour (recommended), or visitor centre + exterior only?
