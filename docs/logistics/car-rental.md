## Value Assessment

Reviewed Jun 2026. Current market rate for a comparable estate car with full coverage at Dublin Airport in peak July is **€60–80/day** (Skyscanner average: ~€74/day). The Avis pre-paid rate works out to **~€47.5/day fully inclusive** — well below market at this late stage.

**Verdict: no action needed.** 6 weeks before peak-season travel, all alternatives are more expensive. The pre-paid February booking captured the best available rate.

## Notes

**Third-party excess insurance (recommended):** The €3,500 CDW and theft excess is a meaningful exposure. A third-party excess policy costs ~€5–8/day (~€50–80 for 10 days) and reduces your excess to zero if the car is damaged or stolen. Providers: [iCarhireinsurance.com](https://www.icarhireinsurance.com/), [MoneyMaxim](https://www.moneymaxim.co.uk/car-hire-excess-insurance/), or check if your travel insurance already covers it.

**Northern Ireland crossing:** The route includes Northern Ireland (Belfast leg). The booking confirmation lists a "Cross Border Fee" with no separate charge — confirm with Avis whether NI crossings are permitted under this rate and if any declaration is needed at pick-up.

**Excess waiver at counter:** Avis will offer their own Super Cover product at pick-up. This is typically overpriced (€25–35/day) compared to third-party excess insurance — decline it if you have third-party cover.
