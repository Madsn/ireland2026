# Glendalough

## What It Is
Early Christian monastic settlement founded by St Kevin in the 6th century, set in a dramatic glacial valley in the Wicklow Mountains. One of Ireland's most visited heritage sites.

## Why We're Interested
Listed as the main activity for the Wicklow leg (Jul 21–22).

## Key Facts
- Located in Co. Wicklow, ~1h south of Dublin city
- Free to walk the valley and monastic site; parking fee applies
- The famous Round Tower, cathedral ruins, and two lakes (Upper and Lower)
- Druids Glen Resort (the hotel) is ~20 min away

## Notes & Tips
- Allow at least 2–3 hours; longer if hiking
- Upper Lake trail is the most scenic walk (~1h loop)
- Can get crowded mid-day in summer — arrive early if possible
- Combine with a drive through Wicklow Mountains National Park
