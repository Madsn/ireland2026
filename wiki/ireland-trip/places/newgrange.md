# Newgrange / Brú na Bóinne

## What It Is
UNESCO World Heritage Site. Neolithic passage tomb built ~3200 BC — older than Stonehenge and the Pyramids. Includes Newgrange, Knowth, and Dowth.

## Why We're Interested
Identified in the planning doc as a key stop on the Drogheda leg.

## Key Facts
- Access is via the **Brú na Bóinne Visitor Centre** — you cannot walk to the monuments independently
- All visits go by shuttle bus from the visitor centre
- **Must pre-book in summer** — sells out weeks in advance
- Opening hours vary by season; check OPW website

## Notes & Tips
- Book the combined Newgrange + Knowth ticket for best value
- Allow 2.5–3 hours for the full visit
- The solstice sunrise alignment (Dec 21) is famous — not relevant in July but worth mentioning to kids
