# Emerald Park

Theme park and zoo in Ashbourne, Co. Meath (formerly known as Tayto Park). Rollercoasters, rides, and a zoo — Ireland's largest theme park. An entire leg is built around visiting this.

## Booking Notes

⚠️ **Book tickets on July 20 for July 21** to secure the 20% early-booking discount. Do not leave this to the day of the visit — the discount may not apply on-the-day.

## Notes

- Full day out — plan for 6–8 hours
- July is peak Irish school holiday season — gets busy
- Check height requirements for rides
