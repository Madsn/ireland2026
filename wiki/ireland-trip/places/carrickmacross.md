# Carrickmacross Workhouse

## What It Is
Famine-era workhouse museum in Carrickmacross, Co. Monaghan. Documents the experience of the Irish Famine (1845–1852) through the lens of a local workhouse.

## Why We're Interested
Planning doc flags this as a **must-see for our kids**.

## Key Facts
- Located in Carrickmacross, Co. Monaghan — slightly off the Dublin–Drogheda main route
- Detour: ~30 min off the N2/M2 route (or add as a scenic alternative route)
- Check opening hours before visiting — smaller heritage sites sometimes have limited hours

## Notes & Tips
- Powerful educational experience for children; well-suited to the ages noted (~8)
- Carrickmacross is also known for Carrickmacross lace — craft shops if interested
