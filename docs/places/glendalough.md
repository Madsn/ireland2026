# Glendalough

Early Christian monastic settlement founded by St Kevin in the 6th century, set in a dramatic glacial valley in the Wicklow Mountains. One of Ireland's most visited heritage sites.

## Key Facts

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| County | Wicklow |
| Distance from Dublin | ~1h south |
| Entry | Free to walk the valley and monastic site; parking fee applies |
| Time needed | 2–3 hours minimum |
| Best walk | Upper Lake trail (~1h loop, most scenic) |
| Nearest hotel | [Druids Glen Resort](../logistics/druids-glen-resort.md) (~20 min) |

## Notes

- Famous Round Tower, cathedral ruins, and two lakes (Upper and Lower)
- Can get crowded mid-day in summer — arrive early if possible
- Combine with a drive through Wicklow Mountains National Park
- Beautiful even in rain
