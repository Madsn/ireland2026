    Still to book¶

    1. Brú na Bóinne / Newgrange — book 16 June 2026 ⚠️¶

    OPW bookings open exactly 30 days ahead, so the Jul 16 window opens Jun 16. July slots go within hours — set a reminder and book the moment it opens at heritageireland.ie. Target a ~13:30 afternoon slot. Strictest cancellation of all: refundable only within 1 hour of purchase; no amendments or transfers after.

    Book the Brú na Bóinne Tour + Newgrange Chamber ticket:

    Ticket Includes Verdict
    Brú na Bóinne Tour + Newgrange Chamber Visitor centre + shuttle + guided entry into the passage-tomb chamber ✅ Book this. The chamber interior is the whole reason Newgrange is a UNESCO site
    Newgrange Tour + Exhibition Visitor centre + shuttle + exterior only ✗ Skip — no chamber access
    Knowth Tour + Exhibition Visitor centre + Knowth exterior ✗ Skip — no time for both sites in the afternoon window
