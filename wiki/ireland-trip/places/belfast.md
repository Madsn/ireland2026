# Belfast

## What It Is
Capital of Northern Ireland. Industrial and maritime heritage city, famous for the Titanic.

## Why We're Interested
2-night base (Jul 18–20) for the Ulster leg.

## Key Facts
- Hotel: [[logistics/ormeau-park-residence]] (Ormeau Park, south Belfast)
- 2 nights: Jul 18–20
- Currency: GBP (British pounds sterling)

## Planned Activities
- **Titanic Belfast** — flagship attraction, pre-book ⭐
- SS Nomadic — Titanic's tender ship, adjacent to Titanic Belfast
- Ulster Museum — free, national collections
- Ulster Folk Museum — open-air museum (~20 min from city)
- W5 — science centre for kids
- Belfast Cathedral — city centre
- Belfast Castle — Cave Hill, views over city
- [[places/carrickfergus-castle]] — ~20 min north, Norman castle

## Notes & Tips
- Titanic Quarter is a half-day minimum: Titanic Belfast + SS Nomadic + dock views
- W5 is excellent for kids ~5–12
- Ormeau Park is in south Belfast, 15–20 min walk from city centre
