# Drogheda

## What It Is
Historic town on the River Boyne, Co. Louth. Close to Newgrange and other Boyne Valley sites.

## Why We're Interested
1-night stop (Jul 16–17) en route north. Key draw is Brú na Bóinne (Newgrange passage tomb).

## Key Facts
- Hotel: [[logistics/scholars-townhouse-hotel]]
- Distance from Dublin: ~50 km / 45 min
- Distance from NI border: ~75 km

## Planned Activities
- [[places/newgrange]] — passage tombs, Brú na Bóinne visitor centre (**pre-book**)
- Drogheda Museum
- Irish Military War Museum
- Old Mellifont Abbey
- En route: [[places/ardgillan]] (castle and gardens)
- En route: [[places/carrickmacross]] Workhouse (must-see for kids)

## Notes & Tips
- Brú na Bóinne is one of the great prehistoric sites in Europe — book well in advance for summer
- Drogheda itself has a good medieval core worth a short walk
