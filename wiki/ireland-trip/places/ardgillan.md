# Ardgillan Castle and Garden

## What It Is
18th-century castle with gardens overlooking the Irish Sea, in Balbriggan, Co. Fingal. Run by Fingal County Council.

## Why We're Interested
Listed as an en-route stop on the Dublin → Drogheda leg (Jul 16).

## Key Facts
- The castle grounds and gardens are free to enter
- Castle tours available (check for summer hours/booking)
- Near The Heritage Centre (also mentioned in the planning doc)
- About 30km north of Dublin city, directly on the N1/M1 to Drogheda
