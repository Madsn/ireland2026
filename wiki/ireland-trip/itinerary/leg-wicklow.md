# Wicklow / South Dublin — July 21–22 (1 night)

## Accommodation
[[logistics/druids-glen-resort]] · €454.50 · breakfast included

## Route
Emerald Park area → Wicklow Mountains → Druids Glen Resort

## Activities

| Activity | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| [[places/glendalough\|Glendalough]] | Early Christian monastic site — stunning valley, free to walk |
| [[places/wicklow-mountains\|Wicklow Mountains National Park]] | Hiking, scenery, can combine with Glendalough |

## Driving Notes
- From Emerald Park area (Navan) to Glendalough: ~1h15m
- Glendalough to Druids Glen Resort: ~20 min (both in Co. Wicklow)
- Druids Glen to Dublin Airport (next day): ~45 min

## Contingency
Glendalough valley is beautiful even in rain. Druids Glen resort has spa/facilities if weather is poor.

## Open Questions
- How long to spend at Glendalough — half day is enough for a walk around the lakes
- Any specific hiking trails planned? Upper Lake trail is most popular.
