# Emerald Park

## What It Is
Theme park and zoo in Ashbourne, Co. Meath (formerly known as Tayto Park). Rollercoasters, rides, and a zoo — Ireland's largest theme park.

## Why We're Interested
The planning doc builds an entire leg around visiting this. Kids are the primary audience.

## Key Facts
- Located in Ashbourne, Co. Meath — ~25 km north of Dublin city
- From Belfast: ~1h30m drive
- **20% discount when booking in advance** — the plan is to book on Jul 20 for Jul 21
- Tickets are available online at their website

## Booking Notes
- Book tickets on **July 20** for **July 21** to get the 20% early-booking discount
- Do not leave this to the day — discount may not apply on-the-day

## Notes & Tips
- Full day out — plan for 6–8 hours
- Gets busy in school holidays (July is peak Irish school holiday season)
- Check height requirements for rides if kids are young
