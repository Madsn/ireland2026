# Leg 6 — Wicklow (Jul 21–22)

1-night at [Druids Glen Resort](../logistics/druids-glen-resort.md) · €454.50 · breakfast included (adults; children €12.50 extra).

## Route

```
Emerald Park area → Glendalough → Druids Glen Resort
```

## Activities

| Activity | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| [Glendalough](../places/glendalough.md) | Early Christian monastic site · stunning valley · free to walk · allow 2–3 hours |
| [Wicklow Mountains National Park](https://maps.google.com/?q=Wicklow+Mountains+National+Park+Ireland) | Combine with Glendalough drive |

## Driving

- Emerald Park area (Navan) → Glendalough: ~1h15m
- Glendalough → Druids Glen Resort: ~20 min
- Druids Glen → Dublin Airport (next morning): ~45 min

## Notes

- Book Hugo's Restaurant at Druids Glen directly with the hotel
- Upper Lake trail at Glendalough is the most scenic walk (~1h loop)
- Children's breakfast at Druids Glen: €12.50 per child per day (not included)
