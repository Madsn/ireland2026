### Important exclusions

**War and civil unrest (General Exclusion 4):** No cover for any claim arising from war, invasion, revolution, civil unrest, rebellion, act of foreign enemy, or any similar event. Applies to all sections including cancellation and medical.

**Terrorism (General Exclusion 5):** Terrorism is excluded unless the terrorism extension has been contracted. If included, the extension covers Emergency Medical & Other Expenses (Section B) only — trip cancellation due to terrorism is not covered under any circumstance.

**International conflicts (General Exclusion 19):** Claims are excluded for events occurring in countries on the UK FCDO not-recommended list, or in any international conflicts or interventions involving the use of force or coercion. Ireland, Northern Ireland, and Denmark are not affected by this exclusion.

!!! note "UAE/Iran regional risk"
    The war and conflict exclusions mean that disruption to UAE flights caused by a regional military conflict (e.g. US-Iran escalation closing UAE airspace) would not be covered. This exclusion is universal across all GCC travel insurers — no consumer policy covers war-related cancellation. The appropriate mitigation is booking changeable/refundable fares and monitoring FCDO advisories before departure.
